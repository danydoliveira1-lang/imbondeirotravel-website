# Project Baobab — Phase 3B Editorial Destination Stories

This update preserves Phase 3A and adds a full-screen editorial destination experience.

## What changed

- Clicking a destination card or glowing map marker now opens a full-page story.
- Serra da Leba uses the local cinematic video in `/public/videos/serra-da-leba.mp4`.
- Each story includes narrative, highlights, practical details, related Signature Journeys, next departure dates, Add to My Journey, and Craft My Journey.
- “Experiences Worth Remembering / Signature Angola Journeys” is hidden from the normal homepage and revealed only from the full-screen menu.
- “Begin Your Journey” is renamed “Craft Your Journey” and hidden from the normal homepage; it is revealed from the menu or a destination story.
- Signature Departures remains visible as a carefully curated homepage feature.

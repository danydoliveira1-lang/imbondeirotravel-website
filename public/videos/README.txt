PROJECT BAOBAB — HERO MEDIA

This package includes three working, lightweight MP4 hero sequences generated from the preserved Step 2 photographs:
- journey-angola.mp4
- kalandula-falls.mp4
- traditional-dance.mp4

They ensure the cinematic hero works immediately on Vercel and on mobile browsers.

For the final launch, replace journey-angola.mp4 with approved Serra da Leba drone footage using the same filename. You may also replace the other two files with original footage without changing the React code.

Recommended final video format:
- MP4 / H.264
- 1920 × 1080 or 1280 × 720
- 10 seconds per scene
- muted / no audio track
- web optimized with fast-start metadata
- ideally under 8 MB per file
